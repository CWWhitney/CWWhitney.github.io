I build open-source R tools for decision modeling under uncertainty and for ethnobotany, including [decisionSupport](https://cran.r-project.org/package=decisionSupport) (co-developer), [ethnobotanyR](https://cran.r-project.org/package=ethnobotanyR), [evca](https://github.com/CWWhitney/26_ev_ambiguity) and [hcbic](https://github.com/CWWhitney/hill_climbing_bic).
