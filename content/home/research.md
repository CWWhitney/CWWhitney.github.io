My research has three connected themes:

- **Decision analysis under uncertainty.** Probabilistic models, built with stakeholders, that compare interventions before money is spent on them.
- **Agroecology and agroforestry.** Why smallholders adopt, adapt or abandon trees on their farms, and what restoration and carbon schemes deliver.
- **Food, nutrition and ethnobotany.** How homegardens and wild plants contribute to diets, and how communities know, use and value plants.
