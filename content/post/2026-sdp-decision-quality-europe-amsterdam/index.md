At **Decision Quality Europe 2026**, the Society of Decision Professionals conference in Amsterdam (9–12 November), I gave a talk on decision leadership under causal ambiguity and co-presented two posters: one with **Daniel Rozeik** on justice trade-offs in scaling, and one with **Rika Augustin** on probabilistic decision analysis for agricultural development interventions.
