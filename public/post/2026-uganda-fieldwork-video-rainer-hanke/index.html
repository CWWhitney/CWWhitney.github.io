Uganda 2014: a film of my PhD fieldwork by Rainer Hanke

Last updated on Oct 5, 2026 1 min read
Still from the film

In 2014 Rainer Hanke made a film about my PhD fieldwork in Southwest Uganda, Gardens, Markets and communities with Dr. Katja Kehlenbeck and PhD student Cory Whitney. It follows our work on homegardens, ethnobotany and agroecology with farming communities. The film was never published, and I could not put it on YouTube because of the music. Rainer has since passed away. I am glad to finally share his work, which is now hosted at the Internet Archive.

The film is in German and runs about 44 minutes.

Watch or download the film at the Internet Archive
